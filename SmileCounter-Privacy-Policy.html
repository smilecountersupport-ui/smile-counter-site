Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 10, 2026

Privacy by design: Smile Counter is designed so that smile detection and facial-expression analysis happen on your device. The developer does not receive, store, or upload your camera images, video, facial geometry, or face-tracking data to developer-controlled servers.

1. Scope and who is responsible

This Privacy Policy explains how the Smile Counter iPhone application (the “App”) and the Smile Counter support website (together, the “Services”) handle information.

For personal data that is actually received or controlled by the developer, the data controller is the person or legal entity identified as the developer or seller of Smile Counter on the Apple App Store (the “Developer,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). You can contact us at smilecounter.support@gmail.com.

Some third parties, including Apple, GitHub, and Google, may process information independently under their own terms and privacy policies. Their processing is not controlled by this Privacy Policy except to the extent applicable law makes us responsible for it.

2. Camera and facial-expression processing

The App uses the front-facing camera and Apple-provided face-tracking capabilities to detect facial expressions associated with smiling and to count smile events.

  • Camera frames, facial geometry, and face-tracking/blendshape information are processed on your device for the App’s core functionality.
  • The Developer does not receive or store those camera frames, videos, facial geometry, faceprints, biometric templates, or face-tracking data on developer-controlled servers.
  • The App does not use face data to identify or authenticate you.
  • The Developer does not use face data for advertising, marketing, tracking, profiling, data mining, or sale.
  • The App does not attempt to infer sensitive characteristics such as identity, race or ethnicity, religion, health status, sexual orientation, political views, or similar traits from your face.

You control camera access through iOS permission settings. If you disable camera permission, smile-counting functionality that requires the camera will not work.

3. Information stored locally on your device

The App may store smile counts, daily history, streaks, goals, onboarding status, reminder preferences, and similar settings locally on your device. The Developer does not receive this local App history through developer-controlled servers.

You can delete or reset local App data using available controls in the App or by deleting the App, subject to Apple’s device backup and restore features and your iCloud/device settings. Because the Developer does not possess this local data, we generally cannot access, export, correct, or delete it remotely for you.

4. Notifications

If you enable reminders, the App may schedule local notifications on your device. Notification scheduling is performed using Apple system functionality. You can change or disable notification permission at any time in iOS Settings and, where available, within the App.

5. Subscriptions and purchases

Subscriptions are offered and processed through Apple’s App Store and StoreKit. Apple handles payment credentials and billing. The Developer does not receive or store your full payment-card number.

The App may receive or verify StoreKit transaction and entitlement information needed to determine whether you have access to subscribed features. Apple may also make sales, transaction, storefront, refund, and subscription reporting available to developers through App Store Connect. Apple’s own processing is governed by Apple’s privacy terms and applicable law.

6. Support communications

If you contact us at smilecounter.support@gmail.com, we receive the information you choose to provide, which may include your email address, name, message content, screenshots, device model, iOS version, transaction-related information, or other details you include.

We use support communications only to respond to you, troubleshoot problems, prevent abuse or fraud, improve the Services, comply with law, and establish or defend legal claims where necessary. Please do not send passwords, payment-card numbers, government identification numbers, medical records, or other information that is not needed for support.

Support email is hosted using Google services. Information you send by email may therefore be processed by Google in accordance with Google’s applicable privacy terms.

7. Website hosting and technical data

The Smile Counter support website is hosted using GitHub Pages. We do not intentionally add advertising trackers or our own analytics scripts to the current website. GitHub states that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor’s IP address is logged and stored by GitHub for security purposes, and GitHub may process other technical information under its own privacy terms.

Because this technical processing is performed by GitHub as the hosting platform, its handling of that information is also subject to GitHub’s privacy documentation and applicable law.

8. Advertising, sale, sharing, and cross-app tracking

The current version of Smile Counter does not include developer-operated advertising, third-party advertising SDKs, cross-app behavioral tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not use facial or camera information for advertising, marketing, or user profiling.

If these practices materially change in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy and applicable App Store privacy disclosures before or when required by law, and we will obtain consent where legally required.

9. Legal bases for EEA and UK users

Where the GDPR, UK GDPR, or similar law applies to personal data that we actually receive, we process it only when we have an appropriate legal basis. Depending on the circumstances, this may include:

  • Performance of a contract or steps requested before a contract — for example, responding to support necessary to provide the App or subscription service.
  • Legitimate interests — such as maintaining security, preventing abuse, troubleshooting, improving reliability, and managing legal claims, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Legal obligations — where retention or disclosure is required by applicable law.
  • Consent — where applicable law specifically requires consent; consent can be withdrawn prospectively where the law gives you that right.

Core camera processing described above occurs locally on your device and is not transmitted to the Developer.

10. Retention

We retain personal data that we actually receive only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was received, including resolving support requests, maintaining security and fraud-prevention records, meeting accounting or legal obligations, and establishing, exercising, or defending legal claims. We periodically review retained information and delete or anonymize it when it is no longer reasonably necessary, subject to applicable law.

Local App data remains under your device-level control as described above. Apple, Google, and GitHub determine retention for information they process under their own services and legal obligations.

11. International processing

Apple, Google, and GitHub operate internationally. Information processed through their services may be handled in countries other than your own, subject to their contractual arrangements, privacy terms, and transfer safeguards where required by applicable law. The Developer does not independently transfer on-device camera or facial-expression data to developer-controlled servers in another country because that data is not uploaded to us.

12. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live and the law that applies, you may have rights regarding personal data that we actually hold, such as rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, obtain a portable copy, withdraw consent, and lodge a complaint with a competent data-protection authority. California and other U.S. state residents may have additional rights where the relevant law applies, including rights to know, correct, or delete certain personal information and to opt out of sale or certain sharing. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

To exercise a right concerning data we actually hold, email smilecounter.support@gmail.com. We may need to verify your request in a proportionate manner before acting on it. These rights are subject to legal exceptions and may not apply to data that the Developer never receives, such as local smile history or on-device camera processing.

13. Children

Smile Counter is a general-audience lifestyle application and is not specifically directed to children under 13. The Developer does not knowingly collect camera images, face-tracking data, or smile history from children through developer-controlled servers. If a child contacts support and applicable law requires parental involvement or deletion, a parent or guardian may contact us at smilecounter.support@gmail.com.

Subscriptions must be authorized through the Apple Account and purchase controls applicable to the user, including parental or family controls where required.

14. Security

We use a data-minimization approach and design the App so that sensitive camera and facial-expression processing remains on-device. However, no software, device, email system, website, or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device, Apple Account, and email account.

15. Automated decisions

Smile detection may automatically increment counters or update in-app progress. The App does not use automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects about you.

For privacy questions, requests, or complaints, contact smilecounter.support@gmail.com.

If applicable law gives you the right to complain to a data-protection or consumer-protection authority, nothing in this Privacy Policy limits that right.
