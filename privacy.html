Privacy Policy

Effective September 10, 2026.

This Privacy Policy describes how Smile Counter handles information. It is intended to be read together with applicable App Store disclosures and does not limit rights that cannot lawfully be waived.

1. Who is responsible

The controller for information actually received by the developer is the person or legal entity identified as the developer or seller of Smile Counter on the Apple App Store. Contact: smilecounter.support@gmail.com.

2. Camera and facial-expression processing

Smile Counter uses the front camera and Apple ARKit facial-expression/blend-shape information to detect smile events. This processing is designed to occur on-device. The developer does not intentionally receive, store, sell, or use camera images, facial geometry, or facial-expression values for advertising, identity verification, authentication, profiling, surveillance, or biometric identification.

3. Local app data

Smile counts, history, streaks, reminder settings, onboarding state, and timing used to limit the optional support prompt are stored locally on your device using Apple platform storage. Deleting the app may delete this data, subject to Apple backup and restore behavior.

4. Optional support purchase

Smile Counter offers an optional one-time In-App Purchase to support continued development. Apple processes payment credentials and billing. The app receives StoreKit transaction and entitlement information needed to recognize supporter status and restore the purchase. The developer does not receive your full payment-card details.

5. Support communications

If you contact support, the developer receives information you voluntarily provide, such as your email address, message content, and any diagnostic details you choose to include. This information is used to respond to requests, maintain security, comply with law, and defend legal claims where permitted. Support email is hosted by Google, which may independently process information under its own terms.

6. Website hosting

The support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The developer does not intentionally add advertising trackers or third-party analytics scripts to the current website. GitHub may process technical information, including IP addresses, for security and service operation under its own privacy terms.

7. Advertising and tracking

The current version of Smile Counter contains no advertising SDK, displays no advertising, and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission. The developer does not sell personal information or use face data for cross-app tracking or behavioral advertising.

8. Retention

Local app data remains on the device until changed, reset, or deleted by the user or affected by device/app backup behavior. Support communications may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for support, security, recordkeeping, legal compliance, or dispute handling, subject to applicable law.

9. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or obtain a copy of personal data actually held by the developer, and to lodge a complaint with a competent authority. These rights are subject to applicable law and lawful exceptions. Requests may be sent to smilecounter.support@gmail.com.

10. Children

Smile Counter is a general-audience lifestyle utility and is not intentionally directed to children as a child-specific service. Apple family, parental-control, and purchase authorization features may apply. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the developer through support communications contrary to applicable law, contact us.

11. Security and third parties

Reasonable safeguards are used for information actually controlled by the developer, but no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed perfectly secure. Apple, Google, and GitHub operate independent services and may process information under their own policies and legal obligations.

12. Changes

This policy may be updated for product, legal, security, or operational reasons. Where applicable law requires notice or consent for a material change, the required procedure will be followed.

13. Contact

Privacy questions: smilecounter.support@gmail.com.
