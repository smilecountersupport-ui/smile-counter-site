Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Smile Counter handles information when you use the Smile Counter iPhone application (the “App”).

1. Camera and face tracking

Smile Counter uses the front-facing camera and Apple face-tracking technology to determine whether a smile is occurring. Camera images and face-tracking information are processed for the App's functionality on your device.

The App is designed so that the developer does not store or upload camera images, video, facial geometry, or face-tracking data to developer-controlled servers. This information is not used by the developer for identification, advertising, marketing, or user profiling.

2. Data stored on your device

The App may store information such as smile counts, daily history, streaks, goals, onboarding status, and reminder preferences locally on your device. The App does not transmit this local history to developer-controlled servers. Apple device backup and restore behavior is controlled by iOS and your Apple settings.

3. Notifications

If you enable reminders, Smile Counter may schedule local notifications on your device. You can disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings or within the App where that option is available.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

Smile Counter Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. The developer does not receive or store your full payment-card information. Apple may provide transaction and entitlement information required for the App to determine whether Pro features are available.

5. Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The current version of the App is designed without developer-operated advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics services. If these practices change in a future version, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated as required.

6. Accounts

The current version of Smile Counter does not require you to create an account with the developer.

7. Retention and deletion

Because smile history and related settings are stored locally by the App, you can remove local data using available reset controls or by deleting the App, subject to Apple's backup and restore behavior. The developer does not maintain a remote Smile Counter account database containing your smile history.

8. Security

Smile Counter is designed to minimize data collection by keeping core smile-detection processing on the device. No method of electronic processing can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but the App is designed to avoid unnecessary transmission of sensitive camera information.

9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App changes. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material revisions are published.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact: smilecounter.support@gmail.com.
