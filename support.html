Smile Counter Support

Answers to common questions and a direct way to contact the developer.

Why isn't Smile Counter detecting my smile?

Make sure Camera access is enabled for Smile Counter in iPhone Settings. Face the front camera in reasonable lighting and keep your face clearly visible.

Does Smile Counter save photos or videos of my face?

No. The app is designed to process camera and face-tracking information on the device. It does not save camera images or upload them to developer servers.

How does the daily goal work?

Smile Counter can track progress toward a goal of 200 smiles per day. Holding one continuous smile should count as one smile rather than many camera frames.

How do I restore a subscription?

Open Settings in the app and choose Restore Purchases. Your subscription is purchased and managed through Apple's App Store.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Smile Counter, and use the available cancellation options.

How do I reset local smile data?

Use the reset option inside the app where available. Deleting the app also removes app data stored locally on that device, subject to Apple's device backup and restore behavior.

Still need help?

Contact Smile Counter support and include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of the problem.
