Smile Counter Support

Answers to common questions and a direct way to contact the developer.

Why isn't Smile Counter detecting my smile?

Make sure Camera access is enabled in iPhone Settings, use reasonable lighting, and keep your face clearly visible to the front camera.

Does Smile Counter save photos or videos of my face?

No. The app is designed to process camera and facial-expression information on-device and does not intentionally upload camera images to the developer.

Is the app free?

Yes. All functional features are free and the app contains no advertising.

What is “Support Smile Counter”?

It is an optional one-time In-App Purchase. It does not unlock functionality. It gives a cosmetic Supporter badge and permanently suppresses the occasional support reminder while Apple recognizes the entitlement.

How often can the support pop-up appear?

It may appear on the first app opening. If you have not supported the app, it can appear no more than once per local calendar day and can always be dismissed with “Not now.” Reopening the app on the same day does not show it again.

How do I restore my support purchase?

Open Settings in the app and choose Restore Purchase. Restoration depends on the Apple Account and transaction status recognized by Apple.
